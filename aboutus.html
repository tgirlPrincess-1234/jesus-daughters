Mission Statement

JD, Who are we?

  • We are the generation that allows God's Spirit to help us think in new ways!
  • We are the generation that takes up the new nature that God has prepared for us!
  • We are the generation that carries God's own nature!
  • We are the generation that leaves in a truly good way that pleases God!

What do we do?

WE:

  • never make the Holy Spirit Sad
  • tell the truth at all times
  • never speak hurtful words to others
  • do not insult others
  • do not gossip or listen to gossips
  • help others with our words
  • are never jealous of others
  • do not quarrel
  • do not yell
  • help others
  • are kind to all, and
  • FORGIVE
